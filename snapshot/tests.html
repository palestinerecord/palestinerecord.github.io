The record, put to statistical test

Hypothesis tests on the data this site publishes: whether the dead are confined to men of fighting age, whether killing continued through the ceasefires, whether the ICJ orders changed the daily death rate, what happened in the West Bank after 7 October 2023, and whether the Ministry of Health’s named list of the dead is genuine. Each test is stated with its null hypothesis, its data, its effect size and its confidence interval, and every figure on this page is read out of the published data file.

H1

Who is killed: age and sex against the population

H1b

Age and sex of the dead: pattern confined to men of fighting age

null rejected

52.1% of the identified dead (95% CI 51.7% to 52.5%) are women, children under 18 or people aged 60 and over, against 75.5% of the population. More than half of the dead fall outside the group from which armed men are drawn: the dead are not confined to men of fighting age.

Null hypothesis
The identified dead are confined to men aged 18-59.
Data
Ministry of Health named list, 72,835 identified dead; PCBS/UNFPA Gaza Strip population 2023 (2,226,544).
Test
Exact binomial test of the share of women, children and people aged 60 and over against zero
Effect
Share of dead who are women, children under 18 or aged 60+: 0.521 (95% CI 0.517 to 0.525)
p
< 0.001 · adjusted for false discovery < 0.001

H2

The ceasefires

H2a

The November 2023 pause and the daily killing rate

null rejected

Reported killings fell from 284.4 a day in the fortnight before the pause to 0.71 a day during it (rate ratio 0.0025, 95% CI 0.0013 to 0.0047). Killing did not stop: at least 5 people were reported killed in the 7 days. Against the upper bound of 40 deaths the ratio is 0.0201, still a fall of about 98%.

Null hypothesis
The daily rate of killing during the pause of 24-30 November 2023 equalled the rate in the 14 days before it.
Data
Comparison window: Ministry of Health / Government Media Office daily series (Tech For Palestine), 10-23 November 2023. Pause: killings reported incident by incident (OCHA Flash Updates #49-#55; AP; Al Jazeera).
Test
Negative binomial regression on a pause indicator, Newey-West errors (lag 7)
Effect
Rate ratio, pause against the 14 days before: 0.00250 (95% CI 0.00135 to 0.00468)
p
< 0.001 · adjusted for false discovery < 0.001
Sensitivity
  • Exact conditional Poisson test: 0.00250 (95% CI 0.000815 to 0.00587), p < 0.001
  • Against the upper bound of 40 deaths during the pause: 0.0201 (95% CI 0.0143 to 0.0274), p < 0.001

The ICJ provisional measures orders

H3a

The ICJ order of 26 January 2024 and the daily killing rate

null not rejected

No detectable change in the level or the trend of daily deaths at the order (level rate ratio 1.006, 95% CI 0.81 to 1.3; joint test p = 0.99). The order was not followed by a measurable fall in the rate of killing. This is the absence of a detectable change, not proof that the order had no effect on any conduct.

H6

Is the named list genuine?

H6a

The identity numbers on the named list

validity not rejected; the 10% fabrication benchmark is rejected

All 72,835 of the 72,835 identity numbers pass the Population Registry check digit, where fabricated numbers would pass about one time in ten. There are 0 duplicate identity numbers and 0 duplicate name-and-birth-date pairs, and the stated age agrees with the date of birth in 72,835 of 72,835 records. The list as published is consistent with registry data. These tests cannot detect a real identity number attached to someone who did not die; that question needs matching against other records.

Null hypothesis
The identity numbers are valid Population Registry numbers (pass the check digit).
Data
Ministry of Health named list, 72,835 records.
Test
Share passing the check digit, with its interval; exact binomial test against the 10% expected of fabricated numbers; decision rule: validity rejected if under 99% pass
Effect
Share of identity numbers with a valid check digit: 1.00 (95% CI 1.00 to 1.00)
p
< 0.001 · adjusted for false discovery < 0.001

Notes

Notes on the data

Where a test needed data the daily series does not hold, what was used instead and why.

H2a

The Ministry of Health daily series cannot be used for the pause: the Ministry had stopped reporting on 11 November, the Government Media Office issued no total during the pause, and the series records zero for every day of it, which are missing reports and not days without deaths. As directed by the editor, the pause-period count was reconstructed from the killings reported incident by incident in OCHA's flash updates and by the Associated Press and Al Jazeera (five deaths in seven days, a lower bound, since OCHA also reports shootings with 'several casualties' it does not break down). The comparison window is the 14 days before the pause, from the daily series. Because the two windows are measured differently, the test is repeated against an upper bound of 40 deaths, the rise in the Government Media Office total during the pause less the 160 bodies recovered from the rubble.
