Tests

The record, put to statistical test

Hypothesis tests on the data this site publishes and on public datasets from the UN, the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics, the Israel Prison Service, B’Tselem, Yesh Din and the Committee to Protect Journalists: who is killed, the ceasefires, the ICJ orders, the West Bank, the named list of the dead, settler impunity, Area C, food prices, detention without charge, journalists and the destruction of buildings. Each test is stated with its null hypothesis, its data, its effect size and its confidence interval, and every figure on this page is read out of the published data file.

Start here: what a hypothesis test is

A hypothesis test asks whether a pattern in the numbers is real or could be luck. It does not prove a claim directly; it asks whether the data would be too surprising if nothing were going on.

Null hypothesis
The "nothing is going on" answer, for example: the ICJ order did not change the daily death rate. It is presumed true until the data make it hard to believe.
p-value
How likely data this extreme would be if the null were true. Under 0.05 (5%) rejects the null. It is not the chance the claim is true, and it says nothing about size.
Effect and interval
The size of the difference, often a ratio: 1 is no change, 2 is doubled, 0.5 is halved. The 95% interval is the range the data allow; if it includes 1, no change is still possible.
Many tests
Some tests pass by luck, so each p-value is also adjusted across every test run (Benjamini-Hochberg, 5%). Both are shown.
"Not rejected"
This does not prove the null. It means no change was detected; check the interval for what was ruled out.

A worked example. First ICJ order, 26 January 2024: the rate afterwards was 1.006 times the rate before (95% interval 0.81 to 1.25, adjusted p 0.99). No detectable change, so the null is not rejected.

Established: the null is rejected

  • The named list is internally genuine: all 72,835 identity numbers pass the check digit (an invented one passes about 1 time in 10), none is duplicated, and ages match dates of birth (H6).
  • The dead are not just men of fighting age: 52.1% (interval 51.7% to 52.5%) are women, under-18s or aged 60 and over (H1b).
  • Each ceasefire cut the rate of killing and none stopped it: ratios 0.0025, 0.15 and 0.039, all adjusted p < 0.001 (H2).
  • In the West Bank the killing rate after 7 October 2023 was 4.7 times the earlier rate (3.9 to 5.6), and settler incidents in 2024 were 3.5 times the 2022 rate (H4).
  • Settler convictions are rare (odds ratio 0.0025 against Palestinians); 66% of Palestinian victims do not complain to the police; 0.94% of Area C building applications are approved (H9, H10).
  • Food costs 3.2 times pre-war prices; detention without charge is 7.9 times its 2008 to 2022 level; journalists are killed at 4.5 times the Iraq war rate and 7.5 times Syria's; destroyed buildings rose at every satellite assessment (H11, H13 to H15).
  • In the 2008-09 and 2014 wars, 57.2% and 64.5% of those killed took no part in the hostilities, on B'Tselem's count (H16).
  • No state withholding recognition of Palestine has a government that has called Gaza genocide; 50 recognising states do. The odds ratio is 34, but the interval (2.1 to 570) is wide: the direction is clear, the size is not (H8).

Not detected: the null is not rejected

  • The three ICJ orders of 2024 were not followed by a detectable change in the daily death rate: ratios 1.006, 0.82 and 1.12, adjusted p 0.99, 0.17 and 0.84. The intervals are wide enough that a modest change cannot be excluded (H3).

What no test here establishes

  • Why a rate changed: a change at a dated event is an association, not proof of cause.
  • Who is missing from the named list: the unidentified, and the dead under the rubble.
  • Any legal conclusion: a statistical pattern is not a finding of genocide, war crimes or intent.
  • More than the data allow: where a result depends on a data weakness, its entry says so.

In plain terms

What the tests show

The named list of the dead is genuine on every internal test. Every one of the 72,835 identity numbers on the Ministry of Health list passes the Population Registry check digit, which a fabricated number passes about one time in ten; none is duplicated; and every stated age agrees with its date of birth. H6a

The dead are not confined to men of fighting age. Just over half of the identified dead are women, children or people aged 60 and over. H1b

Every ceasefire cut the rate of killing, and none stopped it. Killings continued through the November 2023 pause, the January to March 2025 ceasefire and the ceasefire in force since October 2025. H2c

The three ICJ provisional measures orders of 2024 were not followed by any detectable fall in the daily death rate. H3a

In the West Bank, Palestinians were killed at more than four times the earlier rate after 7 October 2023, and settler incidents in 2024 ran at about three and a half times the 2022 rate. H4a

No state that withholds recognition of Palestine has a government that has called the conduct in Gaza genocide; 50 recognising states do. H8b

Settlers who attack Palestinians are rarely convicted. Only 3% of the police files Yesh Din followed ended in a conviction, and an indicted settler is convicted about half the time, against 99.7% for Palestinians in the military courts. H9a

In Area C, under 1% of Palestinian building applications are approved, and an unpermitted Palestinian structure is about two and a half times as likely to be demolished as a settler's. H10b

Food prices rose by about 30% during the total blockade of 2025, and across the war food has cost about three times its pre-war price. H11a

Administrative detention without charge is nearly eight times its 2008-2022 level. H13

Journalists have been killed at more than four times the rate of the Iraq war and seven times that of Syria's, on CPJ's lowest count. H14a

Destroyed buildings rose at every satellite assessment, and about 11,000 more were recorded destroyed after the October 2025 ceasefire. H15b

In the 2008-09 and 2014 Gaza wars, most of those killed took no part in the hostilities, on B'Tselem's case-by-case count. H16c

H6a

The identity numbers on the named list

validity not rejected; the 10% fabrication benchmark is rejected

All 72,835 of the 72,835 identity numbers pass the Population Registry check digit, where fabricated numbers would pass about one time in ten. §10
Method

How the tests were run

Tests
Counts are modelled as negative binomial, after a likelihood-ratio test rejected the Poisson model in every series where it was applied. Time series use Newey-West standard errors with a lag of seven days. Rates are compared with the exact conditional test for two Poisson rates. Two-by-two tables use Fisher's exact test.
Multiple testing
p-values are adjusted for the false discovery rate (Benjamini-Hochberg, 5%) across all the 30 tests with a p-value. Both raw and adjusted p-values are reported.
Caution
A change in a series at a dated event is an association, not proof that the event caused it. Tests on the named list inherit any bias in who has been identified.
What the tests are not
A test of a ceasefire or a court order measures whether the death rate changed around a date, not why. A test on the named list measures the list as published, not the deaths it does not contain: the 1,205 counted dead who have not been identified, and the dead still under the rubble, are outside it.
Limits
The tests are only as good as the data. The Ministry of Health's daily series changed source during the war (to the Government Media Office from 11 November 2023, and back), carries days on which no report was issued, and, from 2025, separates new killings from bodies recovered only on some days. The named list covers the identified dead only. The population baseline is the PCBS projection for 2023, before the war. Where a test depends on one of these weaknesses, its entry says so, and a sensitivity analysis tests whether the result survives it.
