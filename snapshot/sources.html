Courts and tribunals

Judgments, orders and advisory opinions. The highest evidentiary class in this record: findings of fact and law by the bodies competent to make them. Domestic judgments are listed here only where a question raised in this record has actually been litigated to judgment in a national court.

  • Legal Consequences of the Construction of a Wall in the Occupied Palestinian Territory
    International Court of Justice · 9 July 2004
    Advisory Opinion. Held the wall and the settlement enterprise contrary to international law and affirmed the applicability of the Fourth Geneva Convention to the occupied territory.
  • Application of the Genocide Convention (South Africa v. Israel) — Order on Provisional Measures
    International Court of Justice · 26 January 2024
    Found it plausible that the acts complained of fall within the Genocide Convention; ordered Israel to prevent and punish direct and public incitement to genocide. Measures strengthened 28 March and 24 May 2024.
  • Legal Consequences arising from the Policies and Practices of Israel in the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem
    International Court of Justice · 19 July 2024
    Advisory Opinion. The occupation is unlawful and must end as rapidly as possible; settlements must be dismantled; reparations paid. Paragraph 229 found a breach of Article 3 CERD (racial segregation and apartheid).
  • Arrest warrants against Benjamin Netanyahu and Yoav Gallant
    International Criminal Court, Pre-Trial Chamber I · 21 November 2024
    Reasonable grounds to believe both bear responsibility for the war crime of starvation as a method of warfare and crimes against humanity of murder, persecution and other inhumane acts. A warrant was also issued against Hamas commander Mohammed Deif.
  • Situation in the State of Palestine
    International Criminal Court, Office of the Prosecutor · 2021–
    The open investigation covering conduct in the West Bank, East Jerusalem and Gaza since 13 June 2014.
  • Bosnia and Herzegovina v. Serbia and Montenegro
    International Court of Justice · 26 February 2007
    The only ICJ ruling directly on the duty to prevent genocide. The obligation arises the moment a state learns of a serious risk, not once genocide is proven; it is a duty of conduct, and it is irrelevant whether full effort would have succeeded.
  • Prosecutor v. Akayesu (ICTR-96-4-T)
    International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda · 2 September 1998
    The foundational jurisprudence on inferring genocidal intent from a pattern of conduct and from dehumanising speech.
  • University of Bristol v Dr David Miller [2026] EAT 84
    Employment Appeal Tribunal (England and Wales) · 4 August 2026
    Appeal EA-2024-000324-RS. Lord Fairley KC, with Nick Aziz and Dr Gillian Smith MBE. Eight of nine grounds rejected; anti-Zionist belief in the formulation advanced held a protected philosophical belief, dismissal held direct discrimination. Compensation reduced by 50 per cent for contributory conduct.

United Nations bodies

Commissions of inquiry, treaty bodies, special procedures and operational agencies. Mandated, methodologically documented, and published under institutional accountability.

  • Report of the Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the OPT (A/HRC/60/CRP.3)
    UN Human Rights Council · 16 September 2025
    The first UN body to find that Israel has committed genocide in Gaza, and that Herzog, Netanyahu and Gallant incited it. Chair: Navi Pillay.
  • Report of the Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the OPT
    UN Human Rights Council · 23 June 2026
    Second affirmative genocide finding, on the deliberate targeting of children. Chair: Srinivasan Muralidhar.
  • Anatomy of a Genocide (A/HRC/55/73)
    UN Special Rapporteur on the OPT, Francesca Albanese · March 2024
    The first UN mandate-holder report to conclude that the threshold indicating genocide had been met.
  • Report on starvation and the right to food in Gaza
    UN Special Rapporteur on the Right to Food, Michael Fakhri · February 2025
    Concluded that Israel was committing genocide by starvation.
  • Forcible displacement in the northern West Bank refugee camps
    UN Human Rights Office (OHCHR) · 4 September 2026
    Found the displacement of 33,000+ Palestinians from Jenin, Nur Shams and Tulkarem during Operation Iron Wall to be large-scale, long-term and systematic, raising concerns of the crime against humanity of forcible transfer.
  • Report of the Special Committee to Investigate Israeli Practices
    United Nations General Assembly · November 2024
    Found Israel's warfare methods in Gaza consistent with genocide, including the use of starvation as a weapon.
  • Humanitarian situation updates, occupied Palestinian territory
    UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) · continuous
    The operational casualty, displacement and access record. OCHA's verified fatality database covers 2008–2023; the post-October-2023 figures appear in situation updates pending full verification.
  • Integrated Food Security Phase Classification — Gaza analyses
    IPC Famine Review Committee · 2024–2026
    The authoritative famine classification, including the confirmation of Famine (IPC Phase 5) conditions in Gaza governorates.
  • Health system attacks and casualty reporting, oPt
    World Health Organization · continuous
    The Surveillance System for Attacks on Health Care (SSA) record, and WHO's independent assessment of Ministry of Health casualty data.
  • Situation reports, occupied Palestinian territory
    UNRWA · continuous
    Displacement, shelter and staff-casualty data, and the record of the funding and legislative campaign against the agency.
  • Damage assessments, Gaza Strip
    UNOSAT (UN Satellite Centre) · 2023–2026
    Satellite-derived building damage assessments — the independent physical corroboration of the destruction figures.
  • Child casualty and grave-violations reporting
    UNICEF · continuous
    Including the Secretary-General's annual Children and Armed Conflict report, which listed Israeli forces for grave violations against children in June 2024.
  • Report of the UN Fact-Finding Mission on the Gaza Conflict (the Goldstone Report, A/HRC/12/48)
    UN Human Rights Council · 25 September 2009
    Documented the Dahiya Doctrine of deliberate disproportionality as policy rather than error.
  • Database of business enterprises involved in activities relating to Israeli settlements in the occupied Palestinian territory
    UN Human Rights Office (OHCHR), mandated by HRC resolution 31/36 · first published February 2020; updated June 2023 and July 2025
    The only United Nations register of named companies with settlement-linked operations. The listing grew from 112 enterprises in 2020 to 158 in July 2025. It records listed activity, not a legal finding against any company.
  • Resolution 181 (II): Future Government of Palestine (the Partition Plan)
    United Nations General Assembly · 29 November 1947
    The territorial allocation against which every later land figure is measured: 56 per cent of Mandatory Palestine to a Jewish state, 43 per cent to an Arab state, Jerusalem under international administration.

Peer-reviewed research

Published, methodologically transparent research — principally on mortality estimation, where the difference between recorded and actual death tolls is the central empirical question.

  • Traumatic injury mortality in the Gaza Strip from Oct 7, 2023, to June 30, 2024: a capture–recapture analysis
    The Lancet · 10 January 2025
    Estimated the Ministry of Health count under-reported traumatic-injury deaths by approximately 41% over the period studied.
  • Gaza Mortality Survey
    Published mortality survey research · 2025
    Household-survey estimate of roughly 75,200 violent deaths plus 16,300 non-violent excess deaths, implying an undercount of about 35% and some 12,200 missing.
  • Estimates of Gaza mortality and life expectancy
    Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research (MPIDR) · 2025
    Independent demographic reconstruction of the death toll and of the collapse in life expectancy.
  • Costs of War Project — Gaza
    Watson Institute, Brown University · 2024–
    Source of the widely cited 186,000 figure, which applies an indirect-to-direct death multiplier; it is a projection of total war-attributable mortality, not a body count, and is presented as such in this record.
  • Bad News From Israel and More Bad News From Israel
    Glasgow University Media Group (Greg Philo and Mike Berry) · 2004; 2011
    The foundational audience and content studies on Western media coverage of the conflict.
  • The Hundred Years' War on Palestine
    Rashid Khalidi (Metropolitan Books) · 2020
    Documents the Zionist movement's own institutional use of the vocabulary of colonisation.
  • The Ethnic Cleansing of Palestine
    Ilan Pappé (Oneworld) · 2006
    Archive-based account of Plan Dalet and the 1948 expulsions.
  • The Birth of the Palestinian Refugee Problem Revisited
    Benny Morris (Cambridge University Press) · 2004
    The most detailed documentary reconstruction of the 1948 expulsions, by a historian who rejects the ethnic-cleansing framing — cited here for the facts, not the interpretation.
  • Zionism in the Age of the Dictators
    Lenni Brenner (Croom Helm) · 1983
    Source for the Haavara Agreement and Kareski affair material, corroborated against Institute for Palestine Studies and German Foreign Ministry archive records.
  • U.S. Foreign Aid to Israel (RL33222) and successive In Focus briefs
    Congressional Research Service, Library of Congress · updated through 2026
    Congress's own non-partisan accounting of cumulative and annual United States assistance to Israel: $150bn cumulative as of February 2022, $158.7bn as of March 2023, and the current figure of roughly $174bn in nominal terms.
  • United States Spending on Israel's Military Operations and Related U.S. Operations in the Region
    Costs of War Project, Watson Institute, Brown University · October 2024
    Independent costing of the first year of the war: $17.9bn in direct military assistance to Israel plus $4.86bn in related United States regional operations, and $317.9bn cumulative since 1959 once adjusted for inflation.

Legal instruments

The treaty and statutory texts against which the conduct in this record is assessed.

  • Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide
    United Nations · 9 December 1948
    Article II (acts and specific intent); Article III(c) (direct and public incitement).
  • Geneva Convention (IV) relative to the Protection of Civilian Persons in Time of War
    International Committee of the Red Cross · 12 August 1949
    Article 33 (collective punishment); Article 49 (deportation, forcible transfer, and transfer of the occupier's own population into occupied territory).
  • Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court
    International Criminal Court · 17 July 1998
    Article 7 (crimes against humanity, including apartheid and forcible transfer); Article 8 (war crimes, including starvation as a method of warfare).
  • International Convention on the Suppression and Punishment of the Crime of Apartheid
    United Nations · 30 November 1973
    The standalone definition of apartheid as a crime against humanity, independent of the South African case.
  • International Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Racial Discrimination (CERD)
    United Nations · 21 December 1965
    Article 3, on racial segregation and apartheid — the provision the ICJ found Israel to have breached on 19 July 2024.
  • Basic Law: Israel — the Nation State of the Jewish People
    The Knesset · 19 July 2018
    Cited by B'Tselem and Adalah as constitutional confirmation that the distinction between Jews and non-Jews is treated as fundamental.
  • Israeli–Palestinian Interim Agreement on the West Bank and the Gaza Strip (Oslo II), Annex I
    Signed at Washington, D.C.; deposited with the United Nations · 28 September 1995
    The instrument that created Areas A, B and C as a five-year interim arrangement. Thirty-one years on, Area C — 60 per cent of the West Bank, holding every settlement, the Jordan Valley and the aquifers — remains under full Israeli control.
  • Absentees' Property Law, 5710–1950; Development Authority (Transfer of Property) Law, 5710–1950; Land Acquisition (Validation of Acts and Compensation) Law, 5713–1953
    The Knesset · 1950–1953
    The statutory machinery by which property left by Palestinian refugees was vested in the Custodian of Absentee Property and transferred onward to the state. Applied in East Jerusalem after 1967 and upheld, with restrictions, by the Israeli Supreme Court in 2015.
  • Equality Act 2010
    Parliament of the United Kingdom · 8 April 2010
    Section 10 (religion or belief as a protected characteristic); section 13 (direct discrimination). The provisions under which an anti-Zionist belief was held protected in Miller.

Method

How this dashboard was built

No gaps

build.py parses report-final.md into structured JSON, emitting every heading, paragraph, list item and table. verify.py then reconciles every non-blank line of the source against the output; it currently reports zero missing fragments. The Evidence section renders that JSON in full, so what you read there is the report itself, not a summary of it.

Time-series data

fetch_timeseries.py downloads the Tech For Palestine daily datasets (1,070 daily records, October 2023 – September 2026) and aggregates them to monthly series. Their cumulative totals corroborate the figures cited independently in the report: 73,670 killed as of 2026-09-10.

Corroboration

Every statistic must appear in at least two independent sources before it enters the record. Where sources disagree, the more recent and better-documented figure is used and the disagreement is stated. Where a figure is an estimate rather than a count — as with the mortality studies — it is labelled as one.
