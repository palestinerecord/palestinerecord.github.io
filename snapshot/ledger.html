Where the warrant bites

A warrant is enforced by states, one border at a time. Every state party to the Rome Statute is bound by Article 86 to cooperate with the Court and by Article 89(1) to comply with a request for arrest and surrender. The map shades all 125 of them, marks the five that have given notice of withdrawal — each still bound for the year that notice takes to run — and marks the states whose position the record actually documents.
